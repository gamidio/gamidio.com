Cookie Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Local storage is a similar technology that lets a page keep data on your device. This page lists what Gamidio and the services on it store in your browser. How the related personal data is processed is explained in our Privacy Policy.

Gamidio itself

Gamidio’s own pages do not set any cookies.

The games we host ourselves (addresses starting with /play/) save your progress, settings and high scores in local storage on your device. This is necessary for the game you chose to work, so it does not require consent. The data never leaves your device.

Cookies inside games

Games from HTMLGames.com, Playsaurus and mk48.io run in a frame loaded from the provider’s own servers. The provider and its advertising partners may set their own cookies and use local storage there, and some of them ask for your consent in their own window inside the game. We do not control these cookies and do not receive their data. Details are in the providers’ policies: HTMLGames.com, Playsaurus, mk48.io.

How to manage cookies

  • Use the consent window inside a game to manage that provider’s cookies.
  • Delete or block cookies and site data in your browser settings. If you delete the data of gamidio.com, saved progress in games hosted by us is lost.
