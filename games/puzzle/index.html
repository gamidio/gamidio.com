About Puzzle games

Puzzle games are the most played games on the web, and it is easy to see why: a round takes a few minutes, the rules are simple and the challenge keeps growing. On Gamidio you will find the match-3 adventure Mysterious Pirate Jewels, the colourful bubble shooter Maya Bubbles, the hidden object game Amsterdam Hidden Objects, the block classic Tetro Classic and quick brain teasers like Clear the Numbers.

Every game runs directly in your browser on a computer, tablet or phone. There is nothing to install and no account to create, so you can start a game in seconds and come back whenever you like. If you prefer something faster, try Hextris, a hectic block puzzle on a spinning hexagon, or browse the Card & Board games for mahjong and solitaire.
