Best games for mobile

Every game in this list can be played with your fingers alone. Quick puzzles such as 2048, Hextris and Simon Tatham's Puzzle Collection are perfect for a few minutes on the bus, card games like solitaire and Bingo feel natural on a touch screen, and Chess lets you play a full game against a strong computer opponent.

If you want something longer, Shattered Pixel Dungeon is a complete roguelike dungeon crawler, SuperTux and Neverball get on-screen controls, and Coric's Quest is a short classic-style RPG. Your progress is saved in your browser, so you can come back later on the same device.
