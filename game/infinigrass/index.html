About Infinigrass

  • Simulation
  • Best on Mobile
  • Easy Games

Infinigrass is a free, open-source idle game by Nokorpo, made for the Mermelada Jam 2, where it won first place for art and for enjoyment. Your job is simple: grab the grass growing in a sunny meadow and pull it up. Every tuft you uproot earns coins.

Spend the coins on a better pruner, plant barley and brew it into beer, and hire helpers that do the work for you. The meadow keeps growing, so there is always something to pull. It is a relaxing game with hand-drawn art that plays well with a mouse or a finger.

How to play

  1. Press the beer mug to start.
  2. Grab a tuft of grass and drag it to pull it up.
  3. Spend your coins on upgrades at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Plant barley, brew beer and unlock helpers to earn more.

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: grab and drag the grass to pull it up
  • Click / tap the buttons at the bottom to buy upgrades

Tips

  • Upgrade the pruner early: it pulls more grass at once.
  • Barley and beer bring far more than grass alone.
  • The language button on the title screen switches between English, Spanish, French, Catalan and Polish.

Devices

Plays on computers, phones and tablets in any modern browser.
