About Dante

  • Action
  • Puzzle
  • Best on PC

Dante is a 3D adventure by Salvatore Previti that won first prize overall and in the mobile category at js13kGames 2022. Lucifer's infernal delivery service has failed again: a delivery of evil souls fell into an area under construction, and Dante has to take them where they belong, to the eighth circle.

Explore a comic version of hell, pull levers to open the way and collect all 13 lost souls. You can play with the normal camera or in first person, with music by Ryan Malm and Beethoven's Moonlight Sonata, all in a game that originally fitted into 13 kilobytes.

How to play

  1. Choose Play, or Play first person for a first-person view.
  2. Move with W A S D or the arrow keys.
  3. Pull levers with E or a click to open the way.
  4. Find all 13 souls shown in the counter (XIII).

Controls

  • W A S D or arrow keys: move
  • E or click: pull levers
  • Esc: menu

Tips

  • The soul counter in the corner shows how many are left.
  • If you are lost, try the other camera mode from the menu.
  • Restart in the menu starts the game again from the beginning.

Devices

Plays on computers; works on phones and tablets with some limits.
