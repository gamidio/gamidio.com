About Traffic Control

  • Skill

Traffic Control puts you in charge of a busy crossroads: switch the traffic lights at the right moment and keep the cars moving without a single crash.

Cars arrive from every direction and the traffic gets heavier as you go. Click a traffic light to change it. One collision and it is game over.

How to play

  1. Watch the cars coming to the crossroads.
  2. Click a traffic light to switch it.
  3. Let traffic through without collisions.
  4. Keep going as long as you can.

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: click or tap a traffic light to switch it

Tips

  • Let long queues go first.
  • Never give green to two crossing directions.
  • Watch fast vehicles — they arrive sooner than you think.

Devices

Plays on computers, phones and tablets in any modern browser.
