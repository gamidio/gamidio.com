About Mahjong Connect Jungle

  • Card & Board
  • Best on Mobile
  • Easy Games

Mahjong Connect Jungle is mahjong connect in a lush jungle: link pairs of identical tiles with a path of no more than two 90-degree turns and clear the board.

The tiles lie in one flat layer, and a pair can be removed only if the connecting path is free of other tiles. The jungle setting and the ticking clock keep things lively. Use hints when you get stuck to advance to the next level.

How to play

  1. Find two identical tiles.
  2. Make sure a path with at most two turns connects them.
  3. Click both to remove them.
  4. Remove all tiles to advance.

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: click or tap tiles to select them

Tips

  • Start at the edges, where paths can run around the board.
  • Keep hints for the end of a level, when pairs are hardest to spot.
  • Clear tiles that block long straight lines.

Devices

Plays on computers, phones and tablets in any modern browser.
