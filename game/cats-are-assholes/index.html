Cats are Assholes is a shelter simulation game by Sébastien Bénard (deepnight), the designer of Dead Cells, made in three days for Ludum Dare 40 on the theme "The more you have, the worse it is". You are an old lady with a house full of stray cats, and all you have to do is take good care of them.

The cats want fish in their bowls and clean litter boxes, and more of them keep arriving. Keep the fridge stocked, buy equipment, upgrade the fridge and send your grandson Mark to help. Fail, and the cats eat you. In the Ludum Dare 40 jam the game placed 11th for graphics, 12th for humor and 13th overall.

How to play

  1. Walk around the house with the arrow keys.
  2. Press Space to take fish from the fridge and fill the cats' bowls.
  3. Empty the litter boxes before the cats get angry.
  4. Press C to give Mark an order, and buy equipment as more cats arrive.

Controls

  • Touch screen: joystick on the left, A = use, B = order Mark, X = dash, ↺ = restart
  • Arrow keys: move
  • Space: use
  • C: give Mark an order
  • Ctrl: dash
  • M: music
  • R: restart

Tips

  • Keep the fridge full: running out of fish is the fastest way to lose.
  • Let Mark take care of one job so you can focus on another.
  • Dash with Ctrl when several cats need you at once.

Devices

Plays on computers; works on phones and tablets with some limits.
