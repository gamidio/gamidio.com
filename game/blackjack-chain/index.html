BlackJack Chain is a card puzzle based on blackjack: connect neighbouring cards on the table into a chain that adds up to exactly 21.

Start on any card and draw a path through cards above, below or beside it, adding their values as you go. An Ace counts as 1 or 11, which gives you room to adjust. Make chains of exactly 21 to clear the cards and score.

How to play

  1. Press on a card to start a chain.
  2. Move over neighbouring cards above, below or beside it.
  3. Make the total exactly 21, counting an Ace as 1 or 11.
  4. Release to remove the chain and score.

Controls

  • Mouse: hold the button and move over the cards
  • Touch: drag your finger over the cards

Tips

  • Small cards let you build longer chains.
  • Use Aces to fix a total that is 10 too high or too low.
  • Plan the last card of the chain before you start.

Devices
