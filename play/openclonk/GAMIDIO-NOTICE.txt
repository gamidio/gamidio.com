Changes made by Gamidio:
  - scripts in apply_patches.py: WebGL 2 and GLSL ES, browser main loop, no network play, mod browser or Exit button in the menu
  - build.sh: Emscripten 6.0.6, game data in packed groups, music re-encoded at a lower bitrate; only the System, Graphics, Material, Sound, Music, Objects, Decoration, Arena, Worlds, Tutorials and Missions groups are included
  - index.html: Gamidio launch page; players and settings saved in the browser (IndexedDB)
  - (build output) index.html: no connections to third-party servers; keyboard focus on click; data stored in parts
  - (build output) index.html: on-screen gamepad for touch screens (gamidio-gamepad.js, D-048)
  - gamidio-gamepad.js: Gamidio's on-screen gamepad (written by Gamidio, part of these changes), included in full in this folder
