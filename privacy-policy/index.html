Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you visit gamidio.com, why, and what your rights are. We keep it short because we collect very little: there are no user accounts, no registration, no comments and nothing to buy on Gamidio.

Who is responsible

The controller of personal data processed through gamidio.com is the operator of the Gamidio website (“Gamidio”, “we”, “us”). For anything related to your personal data, write to contact@gamidio.com.

We are not required to appoint a data protection officer.

In short

  • We do not ask for your name, e-mail or any other details to play.
  • Our hosting provider receives your IP address when you load a page, as every website’s server does.
  • Games are provided by third parties. When you open a game, your browser connects to that provider, which may show its own ads and ask for its own consent.
  • If you e-mail us, we use your message only to reply.

What we process and why

Visiting the website (hosting)

Gamidio is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). When your browser requests a page, GitHub’s servers necessarily receive technical data: your IP address, the requested address, date and time, browser and device information. GitHub uses this data to deliver the site and to keep the service secure, and may keep server logs for that purpose. We do not receive these logs and do not use them to identify visitors.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More information: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Games from third-party providers

Most games on Gamidio are embedded from the servers of the companies that make them. When you open a game page, your browser loads the game directly from the provider, which receives your IP address and device data and may use cookies inside the game window. Some providers show their own ads and their own consent window inside the game. These providers decide for themselves how they process your data and are responsible for it as independent controllers:

  • HTMLGames.com (Zygomatic, the Netherlands) — games and in-game ads, including ads by its partners such as Yolla Media: privacy policy.
  • Playsaurus (Clicker Heroes, Grindcraft, Mr. Mine, Poker Quest, Fray Fight) — games, optional accounts and in-game purchases on their own sites: privacy policy.
  • Softbear Studios (mk48.io) — online multiplayer game with its own ads and consent window: privacy policy.

We embed these games because you asked to play them; we do not receive the data the providers collect. To limit it, use the consent window inside the game or your browser’s privacy settings.

Games hosted by us

Some open-source games run directly on gamidio.com (addresses starting with /play/). We removed all analytics and ads from them, so they do not contact any other server. They save your progress, settings and high scores in your browser’s local storage. This data stays on your device; we never receive it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data for gamidio.com in your browser.

When you contact us

If you write to us by e-mail, we process your e-mail address, your name if you give it, and the content of your message to answer and handle your request. Our mailbox is hosted by an e-mail provider in the European Union.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering messages (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); for privacy requests, our legal obligation to handle them (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). Retention: as long as needed to handle your request, then at most three years in case the matter comes up again, unless the law requires longer.

Cookies

Which cookies and similar technologies are used on Gamidio, by whom and for how long is described in our Cookie Policy.

Recipients and transfers outside the EU

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with anyone except the services described above. Some of them are based in the United States or use servers there (GitHub). Such transfers rely on the European Commission’s adequacy decision for the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses, as described in the privacy policies of those companies.

Children

Gamidio is made for teenagers and adults. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before you contact us. If we learn that we have received personal data of a child under 16, we will delete it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased, or its processing restricted;
  • receive data you gave us in a portable format;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing before the withdrawal.

Write to contact@gamidio.com. We will answer free of charge within one month. Because we keep almost no data about visitors, for data collected by game providers please also contact them directly using the links above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live. In Slovakia it is the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky), Hraničná 12, 820 07 Bratislava 27, dataprotection.gov.sk.

No automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Changes to this policy

When we change how Gamidio works, for example by adding a new service, we update this policy and the date at the top.
